Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know
How does Ember ensure food arrives hot and crisp? ▾

We use custom multi-layered thermal heat-locking packaging designed specifically for live-fire dishes. Steam vents prevent sogginess while keeping the core temperature above 65°C throughout transit.

What is your typical delivery time? ▾

Our average delivery time is between 20 to 35 minutes depending on your location. Live-fire kitchens prepare orders in high-heat ovens for lightning-fast turnaround.

Are pure vegetarian dishes prepared separately? ▾

Yes! All our partner kitchens maintain strictly dedicated tandoor skewers, prep stations, and cookware for pure vegetarian preparations to prevent cross-contamination.
